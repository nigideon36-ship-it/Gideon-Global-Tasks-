Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum payout balance and how are payments sent?

The minimum withdrawal threshold is $20.00. Payouts are transferred with 0% platform transaction fees directly to verified M-PESA mobile lines, Airtel / MTN MoMo, and East Africa bank accounts. Withdrawals are processed within 24–48 business hours.

Do I need prior technical training to start completing tasks?

No prior technical experience is required. All task categories include step-by-step instructions, sample golden records, and interactive validation prompts. As long as you follow the task guidelines accurately, you will earn rewards immediately.

How do worker passes (Starter, Pro, VIP) function?

Worker passes (KES 50 Starter, KES 100 Pro, and KES 200 VIP Taskmaster, converted cleanly into each East African local currency) grant access to specialized task categories, increase daily submission caps, provide higher payout multipliers, and grant priority review in the automated consensus queue.

How does the platform prevent fraud and automated bot abuse?

We implement honeypot questions, behavioral timing heuristics, cross-contributor consensus validation, and device fingerprinting to ensure that only legitimate human effort is rewarded. Bot accounts and VPN abusers are automatically quarantined.

Which countries and territories are supported?

Gideon Global Tasks is accessible exclusively across East Africa: Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Ethiopia, and Somalia, with native M-PESA, Airtel / MTN MoMo, and local East Africa Card support.

Are earnings taxable in my local jurisdiction?

Contributors operate as independent contractors. You are responsible for declaring crowdsourced micro-income in accordance with your local tax authority guidelines. We provide annual earnings export statements for your records.
